# WatchTower 0.8.0

Steward adds clear operational ownership and application context while improving sign-in control and audit visibility.

## Highlights

- Assign reusable owners to applications and workspaces through searchable selection dialogs.
- Create owners while editing an application or managing workspace notifications, with unique email enforcement and administrator-only editing and removal.
- Route workspace notifications to the email addresses of its assigned owners, including automatic migration of existing notification recipients.
- Record application criticality, environment, exposure, and operational tags alongside vulnerability and lifecycle findings.
- Optionally prompt users to select an OIDC account or sign in again while preserving seamless SSO by default.
- Separate system, feed, audit, and authentication activity into dedicated views and independently rotating logs.
- Show concise identity-group matching, WatchTower role names, unmatched provider values, and group-overage status for sign-ins.
