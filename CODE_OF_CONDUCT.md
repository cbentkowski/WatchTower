# Code of conduct

## Our standard

WatchTower contributors and participants must communicate respectfully,
disagree constructively, and keep discussions focused on the project. Welcome
people regardless of experience, identity, background, or role, and assume good
intent while addressing harmful behavior directly.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate
disruption, publication of another person's private information, and sexualized
conduct are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Project spaces

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release
coordination, and other spaces where someone is representing WatchTower.
Technical criticism should address the work rather than the person.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful to the project or
its community. Enforcement decisions will consider the context, severity,
history, and effect of the behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner using the contact
method listed on the owner's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue that
identifies people involved in a conduct report.
